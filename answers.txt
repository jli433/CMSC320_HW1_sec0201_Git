Q3: What is the difference between Merging and Rebasing?

Answer: The difference between merging and rebasing is that merging is a safer way to integrate changes from multiple branches while perserving the history, therefore creating a new merge commit. Rebasing instead rewrites the history, putting one branches commits on top of another.
